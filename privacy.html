Darcy privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Darcy is a private, single-user application used only by its owner, Advent Phang.

What data Darcy accesses

With the owner's consent, Darcy accesses the owner's own Google account data, such as Gmail messages and Google Docs, through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide personal assistant functions to the owner, such as reading, summarising and organising his own email and documents. It is not used for advertising.

Sharing

Darcy does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties.

Storage and retention

Data is processed on the owner's own devices and kept only as long as the owner needs it. The owner can revoke Darcy's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Darcy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
